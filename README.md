# Global Air Pollution Analysis: Key Insights, Recommendations, and Data-Driven Solutions

## Project Overview

This project was developed as part of a practical data analytics challenge during my participation in the 3 Million Technical Talent (3MTT) Programme.

The objective of the challenge was to analyze global air pollution data, uncover meaningful environmental insights, and propose practical solutions that support informed decision-making.

Using Microsoft Excel for data preparation and Power BI for data analysis and visualization, this project explores pollution patterns across countries and cities while identifying critical environmental challenges and opportunities for intervention.

To extend the impact of the analysis beyond visualization, the project also introduces AirGuard, an interactive environmental intelligence platform designed to help users monitor, analyze, report, and learn about air pollution.


## Tools Used

# Key Findings

### Air Pollution Hotspots Concentrated in Asia

The analysis revealed that the highest PM2.5 concentration levels are predominantly clustered within the Asian region, highlighting a significant environmental and public health concern.

### Significant Variations Between Cities

Even within the same country, pollution levels varied considerably. Several cities recorded severe pollution levels despite their country's average appearing moderate, emphasizing the importance of city-level analysis.

### Dynamic Pollution Trends Over Time

Pollution levels demonstrated notable fluctuations across different periods, suggesting the influence of industrial activities, urbanization, seasonal factors, and environmental policies.

### Population Is Not the Sole Driver

The findings indicate that population size alone does not determine pollution levels. Multiple environmental, economic, and infrastructural factors contribute to pollution risk.

### Majority of Cities Fall Within Moderate-to-Severe Categories

Pollution categorization showed that a substantial proportion of cities operate within moderate to severe pollution levels, underscoring the need for proactive intervention measures.

# Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations were identified:

- Strengthen environmental regulations and enforcement in high-risk regions.
- Promote sustainable urban planning to reduce exposure to harmful pollutants.
- Increase public awareness campaigns focused on pollution reduction and environmental responsibility.
- Expand access to real-time air quality monitoring systems for citizens, policymakers, and environmental agencies.
- Encourage data-driven environmental policy development using continuous monitoring and analytics.
